How it works

Each player has an ability estimated from fall 2026 results (Terrapin Open @ USC, ACF Fall @ Claremont, BRUIN V: MONSTER @ UCLA) and 2025–26 season stats, fit on the actual teams they played on so that teammates sharing tossups is accounted for.

In a game, each team's chance to win a tossup grows with its players' combined ability, with diminishing returns as teammates overlap; some tossups go dead. Bonus conversion is fit to team PPB at MONSTER. Negs use each player's own neg rate. The four strongest players on each roster play every game.

Prelim standings: wins, then points per game. Top bracket ranks on playoff games only; bottom bracket carries over prelim games between bracket-mates. Players with no record are treated as a typical new player, and set difficulty is assumed to be MONSTER-level.
